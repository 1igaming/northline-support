Northline privacy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy covers the Northline solitaire games, including Solitaire: Anchor, Spider Solitaire: Veer, FreeCell: Berth, and Pyramid Solitaire: Lintel. The games do not create an account, and they do not send the deal, your moves, or your records to a server we operate.

What stays on this device

  • The current deal.
  • Your best time and move count for that game.
  • Whether you have purchased Remove Ads. Apple handles the purchase. The game keeps a local record so the banner stays off after you buy it or restore it.

Advertising

A game can show a banner under the cards and, between deals, a full-screen advertisement. The current versions do not include an advertising network, so those placements are labeled placeholders and do not contact an ad company. If a later version adds an advertising network, this page will be updated before that version ships. Remove Ads turns the placements off.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or phone number
  • No location
  • No advertising identifier in these versions
  • No analytics or crash reports sent to us

Children

These are card games rated 4+. They are not directed at collecting information from children, and they collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
