Northline Anchor privacy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Northline Anchor is a solitaire game. It does not create an account, and it does not send the deal, your moves, or your records to a server we operate.

What stays on this device

  • The current deal, including which cards are face up and which column is the anchor.
  • Your draw setting (one card or three) and your best time and move count.
  • Whether you have purchased Remove Ads. The purchase itself is handled by Apple. We keep a local record so the banner stays off after you buy it or restore it.

Advertising

The game can show a banner under the cards and, between deals, a full-screen advertisement. This version does not include an advertising network, so those placements are labeled placeholders and do not contact an ad company. If a later version adds an advertising network, this page will be updated before that version ships. Remove Ads turns the placements off.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or phone number
  • No location
  • No advertising identifier in this version
  • No analytics or crash reports sent to us

Children

Northline Anchor is a card game rated 4+. It is not directed at collecting information from children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
