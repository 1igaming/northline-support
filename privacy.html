Northline privacy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy covers the Northline games, including Solitaire: Anchor, Spider Solitaire: Veer, FreeCell: Berth, Pyramid Solitaire: Lintel, Sudoku: Seam, Block Puzzle: Keel, and Block Puzzle: Coffer. The games do not create an account, and they do not send the board, your moves, or your records to a server we operate.

What stays on this device

  • The current deal.
  • Your best time and move count for that game.
  • Whether you have purchased Remove Ads. Apple handles the purchase. The game keeps a local record so the banner stays off after you buy it or restore it.

Advertising

The current builds show a labeled advertisement placeholder and do not contact an ad company. Solitaire: Anchor has an AdMob app ready. When a build includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, AdMob may receive the device’s advertising identifier, coarse location, and ad interactions in order to serve and measure ads. Remove Ads turns those placements off. This page will name the SDK version before that build is submitted, and the App Store privacy answers will be updated in the same step.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or phone number
  • No location
  • No advertising identifier in these versions
  • No analytics or crash reports sent to us

Children

These games are rated 4+. They are not directed at collecting information from children, and they collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
