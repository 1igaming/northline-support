Northline Anchor

Klondike solitaire. One column can build in suit.

How to play

The anchor. Before the first move, choose one column. That column can build down by the opposite color, or by its own suit. Every other column builds down by the opposite color only.

The rest is Klondike. Foundations run from ace to king, one suit each. An empty column takes a king. The stock deals three cards. Settings can switch the next deal to one card. The clock starts when the anchor locks.

Undo and hint are on the table. Undo back to the deal unlocks the anchor so you can choose a different column.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase. It turns off the banner and the between-deal advertisement.

On a new install, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where the game is stored

The deal and your records stay on the device. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account to sign in to.
