Northline

One original play in each game. The board stays on your device.

Solitaire: Anchor

Klondike. Before the first move, choose one column. That column can build down by the opposite color or by its own suit. Every other column builds by the opposite color. Foundations run from ace to king. Empty columns take a king. Draw three, or one card on the next deal.

Spider Solitaire: Veer

Ten columns, two suits. A run moves together when it is one suit. One column, chosen before the first move, may change suit a single time, and then it finishes in the new suit. A king through ace of one suit leaves the table.

FreeCell: Berth

Eight face-up columns. Build down by alternating colors. One free cell, chosen before the first move, holds two cards when they are in sequence. The other three cells hold one card.

Pyramid Solitaire: Lintel

Pair cards that sum to 13. A king leaves on its own. One row, chosen before the first move, pairs to 14, so a king on that row can still leave alone or pair with an ace.

Sudoku: Seam

Each row, column, and box holds the digits 1 through 9. Before the first entry, choose one empty cell. That cell may hold two digits, and both of them count in its row, column, and box.

Block Puzzle: Keel

An eight by eight grid and three pieces at a time. A full row clears, and a full column clears. One column, chosen before the first placement, also clears downward when a row clears.

Block Puzzle: Coffer

A nine by nine grid split into nine boxes, and three pieces at a time. A full row clears, and a full column clears. One box, chosen before the first placement, clears when it fills and returns the piece that filled it.
